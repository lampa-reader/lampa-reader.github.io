Adding books

On a phone: Add a book → Files, Whole folder or Catalogs. You can also open an FB2 or EPUB from Files, Mail or a messenger with “Open in Lampa”. On a computer, drop files or a folder onto the window.

Syncing

Sign in under Account & app on every device. Your place, notes, bookmarks and lists sync by themselves on start, when you come back to the app and a couple of seconds after a change.

Translation and dictionary

Select a word or a phrase. On iPhone and iPad translation works offline; the languages download once. The book button in the selection bar shows what a word means.

Deleting your account

Account & app → Delete account. Everything in the cloud is deleted at once; books on your device stay. See the Privacy Policy.
